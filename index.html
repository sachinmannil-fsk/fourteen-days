What you need to know

Where this has to land

If students leave able to say these six things in their own words, the session worked. Everything else is scaffolding.

1Finishing the work does not finish the stress. The body needs its own signal.
2Scrolling is not rest. It costs the same hour and returns almost nothing.
3Tired hours are worth less. Five depleted hours can produce less than three clear ones.
4The weekend is where the term is won or lost — and it is decided on Tuesday, not Saturday.
5One hour of real recovery mid-week buys back several weekend hours.
6Something will be under-done this year. The only question is whether it is chosen or imposed by exhaustion.

Afterwards: use the phrase "the hours you couldn't reach" in an ordinary lesson next week. One unprompted reference from a subject teacher does more for recall than anything inside the 35 minutes.

How the session works

Task cards score points. Life cards appear to do nothing. They are not nothing — a hidden value decides how many hours each team can access next round, most sharply on weekends. Teams that spend everything on work lose access to their own weekend and cannot finish.

Students must not learn this until the reveal. The session depends on them finding it in the scoreboard rather than being told.

Three questions you will get

"How does scoring work?" → "Figure it out." Nothing more.

"What are life cards for?" → Shrug. "They are in the deck."

"The deadlines are unfair" → "Noted. Round continues." Do not defend the school — the app handles this in the debrief.

"But my phone relaxes me"

They are half right and the app concedes it. Two phone cards: laughing with someone (+10) and scrolling alone (+2). Do not argue during play, and do not add a screen-time lecture afterwards.

Safeguarding

The session ends on the envelope screen — the one that says some students carry things school never sees. Say that one aloud in your own words. Do not invite anyone to share and do not look at any particular student.

There is no exit slip in this version. That means the session has no private route for a student who is struggling but will not say so. Watch the room during the debrief and pass any concerns to the counsellor the same day.

If a student tells you something serious directly: listen, do not react, do not promise secrecy, pass it on that day.
